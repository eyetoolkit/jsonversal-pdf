How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Choose Extract selected (keep the pages you pick) or Delete selected (remove them).
  3. Click thumbnails or type ranges such as 1,4,7-9.
  4. Click the action button and download your new PDF.

Good to know

  • Extracted pages are copied byte-for-byte at the object level — no re-compression, no quality loss.
  • Bookmarks and links pointing to kept pages survive; links pointing to removed pages are dropped silently.
  • Need to cut one big PDF into many files instead? Use the Split PDF tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is my original file modified?

Never. The result is saved as a new download; the file on your disk is untouched.

Can I extract every page into separate PDFs?

Yes — select all, extract, then use the Split tool with a per-page range if you need individual files. Or simply extract one page at a time.

Why is my PDF slow to show thumbnails?

Rendering happens on your device. Very large documents (hundreds of pages) take a moment; thumbnails are drawn at low resolution to keep it fast.
