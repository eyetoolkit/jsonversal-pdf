    How to convert images to PDF

    1. Drop your images into the box above (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF).
    2. Arrange them with the ↑ and ↓ buttons — each image becomes one page.
    3. Choose a page size: Fit keeps each image's exact dimensions; A4 or Letter centers images on standard pages for printing.
    4. Click Create PDF and download the document.

    Tips

    • For document scans, A4/Letter with a small margin prints reliably on office printers.
    • Mixed photo sizes? Fit to image avoids white bars and cropping.
    • Images are embedded at full original resolution — no hidden downscaling, no quality loss.
    • The resulting file is a normal PDF: openable everywhere, no watermark, no branding.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I rotate images that are sideways?

    Per-image rotation is on the roadmap. For now, rotate the photos in your gallery app first, then drop them here.

    Is there a limit on the number of images?

    No fixed limit — the constraint is your device's memory. Hundreds of images work fine on a desktop; on phones, add them in smaller batches.

    Does the order of images matter?

    Yes. Images are added top to bottom, exactly as listed. Rearrange them before creating the PDF.
