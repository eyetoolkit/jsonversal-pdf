How to OCR a scanned PDF

  1. Drop your scanned PDF into the box above.
  2. Choose render quality — higher is more accurate but slower.
  3. Optionally limit pages (default: the first 5, since OCR takes a moment per page).
  4. Click Recognize text, then copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

Good to know

  • OCR adds a text copy — it does not convert your scan into an editable Word document, and it does not modify the original PDF.
  • Already have a text layer? Use the Extract Text tool instead — it is instant and pixel-perfect.
  • Clean, high-contrast scans (300 dpi, straight pages) recognize best. Photos of screens or skewed pages reduce accuracy.
  • For best results on a long document, process it in batches of 10–20 pages.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my PDF already selectable?

Many "scanned" PDFs actually contain a hidden text layer from the original scanning software. Try selecting text with your cursor — if it works, use Extract Text and skip OCR entirely.

Can I get a searchable PDF instead of a text file?

Not with this tool — it produces a plain text copy. Creating an invisible text layer inside a PDF is a heavier operation that is on the roadmap.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, though recognition is slower on mobile processors. Stick to small page batches.
