How to add page numbers

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Pick a position (six corners and centers), a format, a start number, font size and color.
  3. Optionally skip the first page so a cover sheet stays clean.
  4. Click Add Page Numbers and download the finished document.

Formatting options

  • 1 — plain number, the most common choice for reports.
  • Page 1 — explicit, good for handouts.
  • 1 / N — "page X of Y", ideal for contracts and printed packets.
  • - 1 - — classic book and thesis style.

The start number matters when your file is part of a larger document: numbering this chapter from 42 keeps the whole set consistent.

Frequently asked questions

Will the numbers cover my content?

Numbers are placed in the page margin with a small inset, so they sit outside the printable area of typical documents. If your content runs to the very edge, choose a different corner.

Are the numbers permanent?

Yes — they are drawn into the page content itself, so they print and display everywhere, unlike page numbers offered by some viewers that only exist on screen.

Can I number only a page range?

Use Skip first page for the common "no number on the cover" case. Selective ranges are on the roadmap; for now, split the document with the Split PDF tool, number the part you need, and merge again.
