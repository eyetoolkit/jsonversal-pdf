How to password-protect a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Enter a password to open the file (or leave it empty to set permissions only).
  3. Optionally set an owner password and choose what readers may do: print, copy, modify, comment.
  4. Click Protect PDF and download the encrypted copy.

How to unlock a PDF

  1. Switch to Unlock mode.
  2. Enter the password the file currently opens with — or leave it empty to strip permissions-only restrictions from a file you own.
  3. Click Unlock PDF and download the password-free copy.

Good to know

  • AES-256 (R6) is the strongest encryption the PDF standard supports; every modern reader opens it.
  • Permission flags (no printing, no copying) are respected by well-behaved readers — the cryptographically enforced part is the open password.
  • This page loads a ~2 MB encryption engine on first use, then works offline. Large files may take a few seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Why encrypt locally instead of using an online service?

Online encryptors receive both your document and your password — the two most sensitive things about the file. Here, neither ever leaves the tab. For documents protected precisely because they are sensitive, that difference is the whole point.

I forgot my password — can you recover it?

No. AES-256 cannot be bypassed, and this tool does not attempt it. Keep your original unprotected file until you have verified the password works.

Can I re-protect with a different password?

Yes — unlock with the current password first, then protect again with the new one.
