How to rotate a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Pick the angle (90°, 180° or 270°) and which pages to affect.
  3. Click Rotate & Save and download the corrected file.

When you need this

Scanned documents frequently arrive with every second page upside down, and phone scans of contracts or IDs often come out sideways. Rather than rotating each page by hand in a viewer, apply the fix to odd pages, even pages or a custom selection in one pass.

Frequently asked questions

Does the rotation survive copying the file to another device?

Yes — it is baked into the PDF itself, not a display setting. Any standards-compliant reader will show the pages rotated.

Can I rotate pages of a protected PDF?

Password-protected files cannot be modified until they are unlocked. An unlock tool is on the roadmap; for now, remove the protection with your own software first.

Will rotating re-compress my images?

No. The tool only changes orientation metadata and the page transformation — embedded images are copied as-is.
