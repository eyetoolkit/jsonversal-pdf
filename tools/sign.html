How to sign a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Create your signature: draw it with the mouse or finger, type your name for a script-style rendering, or upload an image of your signature.
  3. Choose where the signature goes (bottom right is the classic spot) and its size.
  4. Pick which pages to sign, then click Sign PDF and download the result.

Good to know

  • The signature is added as an image on top of the page — the original text and layout are untouched.
  • Signing "all pages" stamps every page at the same position; use the page field (for example 1,3) to sign only some.
  • The output is a standard PDF that opens anywhere, with no watermark.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove the signature afterwards?

The signature is a normal image object inside the PDF, so a full PDF editor can remove it — just like a signature on paper can be crossed out. Keep your unsigned original until you are happy with the result.

My PDF is rotated — where does the signature land?

The position is calculated from the page's visible (rotated) dimensions, so "bottom right" stays bottom right on screen.

Does this work on a phone?

Yes. The drawing pad accepts touch input, so you can sign directly with a finger or stylus on a phone or tablet.
